STEP is the one to open in Fusion: it carries the faces, their edges and the solid they bound, so what arrives is a body you can click a face of, fillet, sketch on and cut — a recognised hole with a diameter you can change, not thirty-two flats. STL is the mesh again, for printing. Saving STEP converts first if you have not.

View

Every face here is a plane and every edge a straight line, which is what prismatic means. There is no CAD kernel in a web page to recognise that a ring of faces was once a cylinder, so anything curved arrives as many flat faces at the deviation above — a scan or a sculpt is better left alone. Within that, the STEP file carries a real solid: shared edges, a closed shell, faces you can select. It is not a mesh wearing a CAD extension.
